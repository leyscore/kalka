# Drawing and snapping

**Player:** a Figma user. **Start:** `/?test`, a 1440-wide artboard.

## Missions

### Edge to edge

Draw a rectangle and make it exactly as wide as the artboard, flush with both sides, by dragging only.

Watch for: the edges catch on the artboard's edges and center, with a red guide, as in Figma; no need to type numbers.

### Line things up

Draw three buttons and line them up in a row with equal gaps by dragging.

Watch for: snapping to the other layers' edges and centers; equal-spacing guides, if Kalka shows them.

### Something too wide

Draw a shape wider than the artboard, sticking out on the right.

Watch for: whether the part outside the artboard shows or is cut off; Figma clips by default with "Clip content" on frames; the setting that controls it is easy to find.

### Draw outside, drop inside

Draw a shape on the empty canvas, then drag it into the artboard.

Watch for: it becomes the artboard's child (layers panel), keeps its look, and the artboard highlights as you drop.
