# Drawing shapes

**Story:** as a Figma user, I want to draw basic shapes at the size I want, so that I can block out a layout fast.

**Start:** `/?test`, a 1440 artboard.

## Missions

### Exact square and circle

Draw a perfect square and a perfect circle.

- **In Figma:** R or O and drag with Shift held; Alt/Option draws from the center.
- **In Kalka, watch for:** Shift and Alt/Option do the same; the size label shows while drawing.

### Lines and arrows

Draw a horizontal line and an arrow.

- **In Figma:** L draws a line, Shift locks angles to 45°; Shift+L draws an arrow.
- **In Kalka, watch for:** Angle locking works; an arrow is available.

### Polygon and star

Draw a hexagon and a five-point star.

- **In Figma:** Pick them from the shape menu; the point count is in the right panel.
- **In Kalka, watch for:** Both exist and their count is editable.

### Round the corners

Give a rectangle 16 px corners, then a different radius on one corner.

- **In Figma:** Corner radius field, the independent-corners toggle, or drag the corner handle on the canvas.
- **In Kalka, watch for:** All three ways work.
