# Clipboard

**Player:** a Figma user. **Start:** `/?test`, two artboards.

## Missions

### Between artboards

Copy a group from one artboard and paste it into the other.

Watch for: it lands in the selected artboard at the same place, as in Figma; Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+V pastes over the selection.

### Between documents

Copy a layer into another open document.

Watch for: fonts, images and colors come along.

### From outside

Paste an image from the desktop, an SVG from another app, and a block copied from a website.

Watch for: each arrives as an editable layer where the pointer or the selection is; a web block keeps its look.
