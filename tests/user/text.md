# Text

**Player:** a Figma user. **Start:** `/?test`, an artboard.

## Missions

### Write a headline

Add a headline, make it 48 px, bold, in another font from the font list.

Watch for: T then click or drag; Escape leaves editing and keeps the text selected; the font loads without the text jumping more than once.

### Edit part of it

Make one word of the headline a different color.

Watch for: selecting a word by double-click; the color applies to that word only.

### Other scripts

Type a line in Cyrillic, one in Arabic and one in Japanese.

Watch for: no missing-glyph boxes; Arabic runs right to left; the font picker says when a font lacks the characters.
