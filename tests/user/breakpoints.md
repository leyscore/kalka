# Breakpoints

**Player:** a web designer. **Start:** `/?test`, a 1440 artboard with a title and two buttons.

## Missions

### Add tablet and phone

Add a tablet and a phone version of the artboard.

Watch for: where the option is; the copies appear beside the desktop with their names and widths.

### Change only the phone

Make the title smaller on the phone only, then change the title's text on desktop.

Watch for: the phone keeps its size and takes the new text; the tablet takes both.

### Remove one

Delete the tablet artboard, then undo.

Watch for: only the tablet goes; desktop and phone stay; undo brings the tablet back with its edits.

### Add a layer on the phone

Add a badge in the phone artboard.

Watch for: whether it appears on desktop and tablet too, and whether that is clear before you do it.
