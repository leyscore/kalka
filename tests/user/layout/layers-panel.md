# Layers panel

**Story:** as a Figma user, I want to read and rearrange the layer stack from the panel, so that I always know what is in front.

**Start:** `/?test`, an artboard with three overlapping rectangles in different colors.

## Missions

### Which one is on top

Say from the panel which rectangle is drawn in front.

- **In Figma:** The first row is the layer on top.
- **In Kalka, watch for:** The same order.

### Bring to front

Bring the bottom rectangle to the front by dragging, then with the shortcuts.

- **In Figma:** Drag the row; Cmd/Ctrl+] brings forward one step; bring to front is listed in the shortcuts panel (Ctrl+Shift+?) for your keyboard layout.
- **In Kalka, watch for:** Dragging above a row puts the layer in front; the shortcuts match.

### Nest and unnest

Move a rectangle into another frame from the panel, then back out.

- **In Figma:** Drop on a row nests, drop between rows does not.
- **In Kalka, watch for:** Same; the frame opens to show its new child.

### Tidy up

Rename one layer, hide one, lock one, select three.

- **In Figma:** Double-click renames; eye and lock on hover; Shift and Cmd/Ctrl select.
- **In Kalka, watch for:** Same; a locked layer cannot be picked on the canvas.
