# Constraints and resizing

**Story:** as a designer making a layout that adapts, I want layers to stay pinned when their frame changes size, so that one design works at several widths.

**Start:** `/?test`, a 1440 header frame with a logo on the left and a button on the right.

## Missions

### Pin to the right

Make the button stay on the right edge when the header gets narrower.

- **In Figma:** Constraints: Right; resize the frame to check.
- **In Kalka, watch for:** Constraints exist and resizing the frame follows them.

### Stretch

Make a divider line stretch with the header width.

- **In Figma:** Constraint Left and Right.
- **In Kalka, watch for:** Same.

### Scale a group

Scale a whole illustration with its strokes and effects.

- **In Figma:** K (Scale tool) or "Scale" constraints.
- **In Kalka, watch for:** A scale tool or an equivalent.
