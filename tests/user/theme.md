# Light and dark

**Player:** anyone, on each platform: macOS, Windows and Linux desktop apps, and a browser. **Start:** the dashboard, then a document.

## Missions

### Switch the theme

Switch from light to dark and back, from the menu and from settings, on the dashboard and in a document.

Watch for: on the dashboard the night sweeps down over the sky and lifts back up, with no flash of white or black, no blink of the whole window, and the window buttons following; in a document the panels change at once and the canvas keeps its own page color. Record the screen: a flash lasts a frame or two and a recording is the only proof.

### Follow the system

Set the theme to System, then change the operating system's appearance.

Watch for: Kalka follows without a restart, with the same transition.
