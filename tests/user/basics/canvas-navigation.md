# Moving around the canvas

**Story:** as a designer working on a long page, I want to pan and zoom without thinking, so that I can move between the whole page and a detail fast.

**Start:** `/?test`, a document with three artboards far apart.

## Missions

### Pan

Move around the canvas with the trackpad, with Space and drag, and with the middle mouse button.

- **In Figma:** Two-finger scroll pans, Space+drag pans with the hand cursor, middle-drag pans.
- **In Kalka, watch for:** The same gestures work; releasing Space returns to the previous tool.

### Zoom

Zoom in on a detail and back out with Cmd/Ctrl+scroll, pinch, + and -, Shift+0.

- **In Figma:** Zoom follows the pointer; Shift+0 is 100 %, Shift+1 fits everything, Shift+2 fits the selection.
- **In Kalka, watch for:** Zoom centers on the pointer, not the middle of the screen; the zoom menu shows the same shortcuts.

### Jump to an artboard

Go straight to the third artboard.

- **In Figma:** Select it in the layers panel and press Shift+2 to zoom to it.
- **In Kalka, watch for:** A fast way exists and the artboard fills the view.
