# Auto layout

**Player:** a Figma user. **Start:** `/?test`, an artboard with three buttons side by side.

## Missions

### Make a row

Select the three buttons and turn them into an auto layout row.

Watch for: Shift+A as in Figma; the gap guessed from their spacing; the row hugs its content.

### Space and pad

Set a 16 gap and 24 padding, then change the gap by dragging on the canvas.

Watch for: the fields where a Figma user looks; the pink handles for gap and padding, if Kalka has them.

### Fill and hug

Make the middle button fill the remaining width, then make the row fill the artboard.

Watch for: Hug, Fill and Fixed where Figma has them; the result matches what the canvas shows after export.

### Reorder in the flow

Move the first button to the end by dragging it on the canvas, then from the layers panel.

Watch for: on the canvas, the drop place shows between items; in the panel, remember the list runs front to back, so the last item in the flow is the first row, as in Figma.
