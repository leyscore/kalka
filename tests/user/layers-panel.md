# Layers panel

**Player:** a Figma user. **Start:** `/?test`, an artboard with three overlapping rectangles of different colors.

## Missions

### Which one is on top

Without clicking the canvas, say from the panel which rectangle is drawn in front.

Watch for: as in Figma, the first row is the layer drawn on top of the others.

### Bring one to the front

Make the bottom rectangle the front one by dragging its row, then with Cmd/Ctrl+] and Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+].

Watch for: dropping a row above another puts it in front; the drop line shows where it lands; the canvas updates at once.

### Nest and unnest

Move a rectangle into another frame from the panel, then back out.

Watch for: dropping onto a row nests, dropping between rows does not; the frame opens to show its new child.

### Tidy up

Rename a layer, hide one, lock one, select three with Shift and Cmd/Ctrl.

Watch for: double-click renames, Enter confirms, Escape cancels; the eye and lock appear on hover as in Figma; a locked layer cannot be picked on the canvas.
